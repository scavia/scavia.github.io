Story mode: finish each level to unlock the next. Moss stops a slide immediately. Pass through a gold sunshard and finish the level to save its memory. Undoing a slide also undoes its pickup. Stars: one for finishing, two within the shortest route + 3 slides, three for the shortest route. Hints and undo have no penalty. Replays keep your best stars and collected memories.

Endless: deterministic puzzle codes and a daily challenge. Every maze is checked for a solution from every reachable resting place.

Settings: choose Auto, 60, 30, or 15 FPS. Lower caps reduce effects and stop idle animation. Timers and movement use elapsed time, not frame counts. Progress is stored locally when available; export a backup to keep it safe.

Five difficulties: the new ladder rates meaningful choices and planning, not route length. Forced runs, immediate reversals and trivial loops do not earn difficulty credit. The grader examines shortest and near-shortest routes from both ends. Rock count alone never raises a rating. Every new mode uses these rules. Story has 60 new fixed layouts, 12 per tier. Daily has a separate puzzle for each local date and difficulty. Old codes and ongoing old layouts are preserved. Earlier stars are archived; unlocks and memories carry over.

This is a preserved earlier layout. Its original rating is unchanged; select a difficulty to play the new puzzles.
What earns a higher rating?
Independent, convincing routes; detours that still look promising after several slides; and choices when reasoning backward from the exit.
What does not?
Extra board space, more slides, fewer rocks on their own, obvious reversals, or long low-choice runs. Alternatives that quickly merge are discounted.
Check more than the shortest route.
The model examines shortest paths and alternatives up to two slides longer. The harder tiers must also meet minimum branching and lookahead checks. The shortest-route number is still your efficiency target, not your difficulty rating.
These are design heuristics, not a measurement of human difficulty. Some puzzles will suit your way of thinking better than others. Every accepted new layout has a route to the exit from every reachable stopping point.
