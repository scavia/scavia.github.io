A gentle guide.
A little orange traveler. A lot of slippery ice. Your destination is the glowing mint portal.
01
Pick a direction. Use WASD, arrow keys, the arrow pad, or a swipe. You can also click ice in your current row or column.
02
Go with the glide. You keep moving until a block stops you. You can queue your next direction while sliding.
03
Land on the portal. Passing over it doesn’t count. Use the blocks to stop in exactly the right place.

Every puzzle is checked for a route to the exit, with no inescapable positions reachable from the start. “Shortest route” is the exact minimum number of slides. Match it to earn a perfect route. Daily drift uses today’s date on your device; share its code to play the same puzzle with a friend.
